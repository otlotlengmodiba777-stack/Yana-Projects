Who we are

Yana Projects was founded on the principle that construction should be a partnership built on trust, transparency and innovation.

We are a team of dedicated engineers, project managers and skilled tradespeople, passionate about turning complex blueprints into lasting structures. Our focus spans high-end residential estates to large-scale commercial developments, always at the highest standard of quality and safety.

We take a client-first approach and use modern construction methodologies to deliver on time and within budget.
